About Us

Dynamflow Store brings you trusted electronics, gadgets and accessories that help you stand out and stay connected. Every item you see on this website is available to order now.

Ordering works in three simple steps: add what you want to the cart, send the order request from the checkout page, and our team contacts you to confirm availability, the final price and your delivery or collection arrangement.

You can always reach us on 0979848479 or WhatsApp 0766628715.

Orders & Tracking

This website does not take payment. Sending the form on the Checkout page sends your order request to our team, who then confirm the order with you by phone or WhatsApp.

To check or track an order, call or WhatsApp us on 0979848479 / 0766628715 with your name and the product you ordered.

Every order you send from your device is also listed in your order history on the My Account page, so you can look it up again. That list is stored in your own browser. Keep the confirmation message our team sends you as well.

Delivery Information

On the product page you choose how you want to receive your order before you send it:

  • Delivery to my address
  • Collect from the store
  • Discuss with me

Our team confirms the arrangement (and any delivery cost) with you directly when they contact you about your order. Delivery prices are not fixed on the website because they depend on your location and the size of your order.

Returns

If there is a problem with something you received, contact us first so we can look into it with you:

Call 0979848479 or WhatsApp 0766628715, or send us a message from the Contact Us page.

Please have your order details (name, product and the date you ordered) ready. We will confirm the options that apply to your item and order before anything is sent back.

Warranty

Warranty cover depends on the individual product, so it is not published on the website.

If warranty matters to you, ask us before you order - call 0979848479 or WhatsApp 0766628715 with the product you are interested in and we will confirm the warranty for that exact item.

Currency

All prices on this website are shown in Zambian Kwacha, using the K symbol (currency code ZMW).

You can also view every price in US Dollars (USD). Click the currency selector in the header (or the K button in the mobile header) and choose US Dollar - every price on the page switches to dollars, worked out from a live exchange rate. Choose Kwacha to switch back. If the live rate cannot be reached, prices stay in Kwacha.

Because no payment is taken on the website, the dollar figures are a guide only. Contact us and we will confirm the exact amount for the items you want.

Privacy Policy

What you send us. When you use our order, checkout, review or contact forms you type in details such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery information and your message. We use those details only to answer you and to process your order or enquiry.

How it reaches us. Our forms are delivered by Web3Forms (a form-delivery service) to the store's email inbox. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

What is stored on your own device. Your cart, your wishlist, your chosen currency, your order history and any details you save on the My Account page are saved in your own web browser on your device, so the site remembers them next time you visit. They are not uploaded to us, apart from the account details you choose to save, which are sent to us so we can help with your orders. A short bar asks you to accept this the first time you visit. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Terms & Conditions

Sending an order request is a request to buy - it is not a completed sale. Our team confirms every order with you by phone or WhatsApp before it is final.

Prices are shown in Zambian Kwacha (K) and may change. Product photographs are for illustration; if an item is out of stock, or the details differ from what is shown, we will tell you before you pay anything.

No payment is taken on this website.

Reviews and messages you send must be your own and must not contain false, misleading or abusive content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the products you want to your cart, open the Cart, then go to Checkout, fill in your details and send the order request. Our team will contact you to confirm it.

How do I pay?

No payment is taken on this website. Our team contacts you to confirm the order and the payment arrangement.

How do I track my order?

Open your My Account page to see the orders you have sent from this device. To check progress, call or WhatsApp us on 0979848479 / 0766628715 with your name and the product you ordered.

Do you deliver, and can I collect instead?

Both. Choose "Delivery to my address" or "Collect from the store" when you send your order. See Delivery Information.

Why are most prices shown as "on request"?

Everything you see is available to order. If a price is not shown for an item, contact us and we will confirm the current price straight away.

How do I contact Dynamflow Store?

Call 0979848479, WhatsApp 0766628715, or use the Contact Us page.
